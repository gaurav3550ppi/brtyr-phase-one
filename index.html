1. Marketplace nature

Brtyr is a service-exchange community and marketplace. Members may act as a Provider (offer services) and as Trade (book services) on the same account. Brtyr facilitates introductions, booking, and in-app credits. Brtyr is not the employer, agent, or guarantor of any member, and does not itself perform the services listed.

2. Eligibility

You must be 18 years of age or older to create an account and use Brtyr. By tapping Save and continue you confirm you meet this requirement.

3. Community Points (CP) and Top-up Credits (TC)

Bookings use Community Points (CP) and Top-up Credits (TC). They are in-app currency only. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, and are not redeemable for money. CP is awarded after a service is completed and confirmed. During a session, CP may show as Pending; after completion it shows as Earned. Providers earn session CP on completion only.

4. Fees and membership

Optional paid memberships (Elite, Premium) and TC packs may be offered. Pack prices and membership discounts shown in Beta may change. Free members pay full pack price. Elite members receive 5% off TC packs. Premium members receive 10% off TC packs. Operating costs for third-party tools stay with the member where noted in-app.

5. Reviews

+5 CP is awarded once to the person who received the service when they leave a review. The provider earns the session CP for the completed booking separately.

6.A Client / Trade responsibilities

As Trade, you agree to book and communicate in the app, arrive (or join) as scheduled, treat Providers with respect, and raise issues through Brtyr’s problem-resolution path. Do not take payments, disputes, or reviews outside the platform when the booking started on Brtyr.

6.B Provider responsibilities

As Provider, you agree that listings, credentials, and photos are accurate; you only accept bookings you can fulfill; you keep availability current; and you resolve service issues in good faith through Brtyr. Off-platform solicitation to avoid fees or credits may result in account action.

7. Problem resolution / disputes

If a trade goes wrong, both sides should first try to resolve it in-app. If that fails, contact Brtyr support with the booking details. Brtyr may pause pending CP, request evidence, and decide a fair outcome (including adjusting or reversing credits). This tap-through does not include a full dispute state machine; the live product will expand this path.

8. Privacy

We use the details you provide to show your profile, match by city / zip, and keep the wallet ledger. We do not sell your personal data. Keep booking, chat, and reviews in the app.
